# SSF screenshot captions

The CurseForge gallery's lines, in gallery order: each shot's file name, its line under
it. One line per shot: the gallery sells, the README teaches. Edits happen here first;
paste from here into the gallery. How to take each shot is in `screenshot_guide.md`.

### 01_in_range.png

Your shard count, on your bag, in one glance. Green means you're fine.

### 02_over_cap.png

Yellow means you're carrying dead weight. One press deletes one, down to the cap you set.

### 03_low.png

Red means go farm before the raid does it for you.

### 04_welcome.png

Install it and it walks you through: every way to delete, and the two macros made for you with one click.

### 05_minimap.png

Count, cap, low mark, paused or not. Always there, never in your way.
