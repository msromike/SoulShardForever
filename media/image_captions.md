# SSF screenshot captions

The CurseForge gallery's lines, in gallery order: each shot's file name, its line under
it. One line per shot: the gallery sells, the README teaches. Edits happen here first;
paste from here into the gallery. How to take each shot is in `screenshot_guide.md`.

### 01_shards_in_range.png

Shows your shard count on your bag in one glance. Green means you're fine.

### 02_shards_over_cap.png

Yellow means you have extra shards. One press deletes one, down to the cap that you set.

### 03_shards_low.png

Red means it's time to go farm shards.

### 04_minimap_and_broker.png

Open the SSF window from the minimap or a data broker. Hover either one for a quick status check.

### 05_welcome_window.png

After install, SSF walks you through the setup and helps you create the macros.
