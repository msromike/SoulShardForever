# SSF screenshot captions

The CurseForge gallery's captions, in gallery order: each shot's file name, its caption
under it. Caption edits happen here first; paste from here into the gallery. How to take
each shot is in `screenshot_guide.md`.

### 01_window_in_range_green.png

The SSF window. Green means you're between your low mark and your cap, and the same number sits on your soul bag, or your far-left bag if you don't carry one. Low shard warn stops at the cap: the track past it is dimmed, with a gold line where the cap sits.

### 02_pause_off.png

Pause all SSF actions is off here, so its sound, volume and chat options are greyed out. They keep your choices for the next time you pause.

### 03_pause.png

Paused, a press neither moves nor deletes, so you can stockpile for raid night. The count goes white while paused: no opinion until you unpause. The pause sound and chat line are optional. Type /ssf to open the window and see your status, and /ssf help for every command. Tab completes the command words.

### 04_match_soul_bag_over_cap.png

Match soul bag size ties the cap to your soul bag's slots (14 here), and Low shard warn can't go past it. Yellow means you're over the cap, so the next press deletes one.

### 05_low_shard_glow.png

Under your low shard mark the count turns red and the bag button gets a red border, so you farm before you run out.

### 06_language_menu.png

Follows your client's language, or pick English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Português or Русский.

### 07_minimap_tooltip.png

The minimap icon's tooltip shows your shard count in its color, your cap, your low shard warn and whether SSF is paused. Click it to open or close the window.

### 08_keybinding.png

Bind Delete Shard to any key. Each press deletes one shard if you're over the cap and moves shards into your soul bag, never in combat. Bind Pause all SSF actions to turn the pause on and off. The Keybindings button in the SSF window opens this page.

### 09_soul_bag_counter.png

Untick Show on soul bag when equipped and the count moves off your soul bag to your far-left bag, and the soul bag shows the game's own number again. Tick it and the count follows your soul bag to whatever slot it's in.

### 10_broker_bar.png

On a data broker bar (Titan Panel, EllesmereUI's data bars and the like) SSF shows your count in its color. Hover for the tooltip, click for the window.

### 11_delete_shard_greyed.png

Delete Shard greys out when a click can't delete, with the reason under it: At cap, Under cap or SSF paused. With help on, its tooltip says why in plain words.
